3D MODEL GENERATION PROMPT — REFERENCE-MATCHED MINIATURE INDIAN FARM VR WORLD

Use the supplied reference image as the PRIMARY VISUAL REFERENCE. The output must be a REAL, COMPLETE, NAVIGABLE 3D environment, NOT a 2D recreation, panorama, background plane, or flat image.

GOAL
Create a polished stylized low-poly miniature Indian rural farming village that matches the reference composition closely. The reference has a compact rectangular farm platform viewed from a high three-quarter/isometric angle. The entire farm is visible together, with crop fields in the foreground, farm buildings at the back, a tall windmill near the center, and tractors/machinery distributed across the right and center.

MANDATORY COMPOSITION
1. FARM PLATFORM
- Compact rectangular/square farm plot.
- Thin wooden fence around the outer edge.
- Brown soil base.
- Several straight dirt paths crossing the farm.
- Keep the entire farm compact enough to see from an elevated overview camera.

2. FOREGROUND CROPS — MOST IMPORTANT
- The front-left/front-center must contain the largest crop area.
- Create many dense, perfectly parallel rows of golden mature paddy/rice crops.
- Next to them create bright green young paddy rows.
- Include additional dark-green crop plots and freshly tilled brown soil plots.
- Crop rows must be long, straight and clearly separated.
- The foreground should visually dominate like the reference.

3. LEFT REAR BUILDINGS
- Large red traditional barn.
- White/cream trim.
- Large white X-shaped double barn doors.
- Small windows.
- Brown/orange pitched roof.
- Beside it, a smaller cream/yellow rural shed.
- Add small farm storage structures.

4. CENTER REAR HOUSE
- Main two-storey blue-and-white farmhouse.
- White/light walls.
- Blue-gray pitched roof.
- Front porch with railing.
- Visible windows.
- Small balcony/upper-level details.
- Place it behind the crop fields.

5. RIGHT REAR HOUSE
- Red two-storey farmhouse.
- Bright blue pitched roof.
- White window/trim details.
- Small porch/balcony.
- Place it to the right of the center farmhouse.

6. WINDMILL — LARGE LANDMARK
- Tall wooden windmill tower directly behind/near the center farmhouse.
- Large circular multi-blade rotor at the top.
- Rotor must be clearly visible from the overview.
- Wooden tower legs and central hub.
- Optional slow rotation animation.

7. WATER TOWER
- Small elevated cylindrical farm water tank near the windmill.
- Tank supported by four narrow legs.
- Clearly visible but smaller than the windmill.

8. MACHINERY SHED — RIGHT SIDE
- Long gray/cream agricultural machinery shed.
- Large open front.
- Several tractors and machines parked inside and outside.
- Include blue, green, red and yellow tractors.
- Include combine/harvester, trailer, plough/cultivator and farm tools.

9. FARM EQUIPMENT
- Multiple stylized tractors with large rear wheels and small front wheels.
- At least 6 visible tractors/vehicles in the overview.
- Place them on paths and around fields, matching the distribution of the reference.
- Add trailers, hay bales, crates, sacks, barrels and equipment.

10. CENTER-RIGHT DETAILS
- Small fenced vegetable garden.
- Small rectangular blue irrigation pond.
- Water pump/irrigation equipment.
- Hay bales and wooden crates.
- Keep these details visually grouped.

STYLE
- Premium polished low-poly mobile-game art.
- Warm, colorful, clean geometry.
- Red barns, blue roofs, green/yellow crops, brown soil.
- Soft shadows and ambient occlusion.
- Slightly hand-painted/PBR materials.
- Cute miniature-farm appearance.
- No people.
- No text.
- No logos.
- No UI.
- No giant empty sky dominating the view.

VR REQUIREMENTS
- True 3D geometry.
- Human player scale approximately 1.7–1.8 m.
- Walkable dirt paths.
- Collision on buildings and large objects.
- WebXR/VR compatible.
- Desktop fallback controls.
- VR controller ray interaction.
- Optimized for mobile/standalone VR.
- Use instancing/LOD for repeated crops and distant objects.
- Keep materials and polygon count reasonable.

CAMERA REQUIREMENT
The initial camera MUST start at an elevated three-quarter overview similar to the supplied reference so that the user immediately sees:
golden foreground crop rows + green crop rows + red left barn + blue/white center farmhouse + windmill + water tank + red/blue-roof right house + machinery shed + multiple tractors.

Do NOT start the player at ground level looking at empty sky.
Provide a second first-person VR camera/mode for walking through the farm.

OUTPUT
Create optimized GLB/GLTF assets and/or a complete scene that can be loaded into A-Frame, Three.js, Unity or another WebXR engine. The result must feel like entering the miniature farm shown in the reference image.
